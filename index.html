Outsource.IO 2016
Outsource.IO · 2016

While working as a designer at Checkmate Creations (later shortened to Checkmate), I was tasked with developing the branding and initial web design for Outsource.IO, a platform designed to connect businesses with the specialists they needed, from artists and developers to copywriters and other creative professionals. The client had just one request for the branding: green. At a time when major social platforms were becoming instantly recognizable by their signature colors, the challenge was to create an identity that felt distinctive in an increasingly crowded app market. The concept of finding the right person to seamlessly blend into a company's team led me to the chameleon logo, which was enthusiastically received by the client. A fun project that tackled brand strategy, visual identity, and early-stage web design.

About

I’m a Portland, Oregon-based designer and illustrator with professional experience in creative fields dating back to 2016. My work spans graphic design, illustration, print production, packaging, digital media, and brand development, with a particular passion for print collateral and visual storytelling. I’ve collaborated with creative teams, led design projects, and worked across a range of industries, translating conceptual ideas into cohesive visual identities and engaging design assets. As a hands-on, collaborative designer, I bring a balance of creative exploration and practical production knowledge to every project. From illustration and image editing to prepress and packaging, I enjoy finding thoughtful, effective ways to bring ideas to life across both print and digital platforms. Outside of design, my work and creative perspective are heavily influenced by the creative culture around me, punk and hardcore music, horror films, tattoo culture, as well as a broader interest in activism, social and political injustice, and environmental consciousness. These influences continually shape how I approach visual storytelling, encouraging me to create work that feels expressive, intentional, and connected to the world around me.
